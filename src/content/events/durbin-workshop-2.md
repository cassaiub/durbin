Durbin Workshop 2 will be a 3-day intensive workshop focusing on observational techniques, telescope optics, and astronomical image processing for astrophotography.

## Topics covered

- Day 1: Introduction to Durbin 2.0, Observation Basics, Telescope Optics, and Intro to Image Processing.
- Day 2: Science Writing (with Dr. Asad), Remote Imaging via iTelescope (with Shahal), and Deep Sky Image Processing.
- Day 3: Advanced Image Processing workflows, Final Presentations, and Evaluation.

## Important Notes

- Attendance: Participants are expected to attend all three days to receive a certificate.
- Equipment: You must bring a laptop to participate in the hands-on software sessions.
- Lunch: Lunch breaks are 1:00 - 2:30 PM (self-arranged, but an allowance of BDT 200 will be provided).

## Participation

The current Durbin Volunteers are all expected to participate. Aside from the Durbin Volunteers, we have 10 seats available for women participants only. Fill the form to secure your spot.

Deadline: January 11, 2026 (11:59:59 PM)
