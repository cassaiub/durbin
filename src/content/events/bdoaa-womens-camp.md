The **BDOAA Women's Astronomy Olympiad Preparation Camp** is an exclusive two-day intensive programme designed to introduce young female students to the core concepts of observational and theoretical astronomy. This camp bridges the gap between raw curiosity and competitive astronomy, paving the way for exciting future Olympiad opportunities.

Registration closes **4 July 2026**.

## About the camp

This exclusive camp is tailored to build the physical intuition and mathematical foundations central to understanding the cosmos. Across two action-packed days, participants will dive into hands-on sky mapping, celestial coordinate tracking, and the physics of stellar light.

## Meet your instructors

Learn directly from an incredible team of accomplished people in STEM:

- **Tahiatun Nahar Juthi**, Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)
- **Labib Bin Azad**, University of Dhaka
- **Mohtasiba Hossain**, Islamic University of Technology (IUT)
- **Fairooz Shaira**, Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET)

## Full camp schedule (Tentative)

### Day 1, Friday, 10 July

Led by **Tahiatun Nahar Juthi**, with **Labib Bin Azad** (University of Dhaka) conducting the afternoon session. The first day kicks off with an essential orientation to the Bangladesh Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics (BDOAA), an overview of the competitive syllabus, and a deep-dive into navigating the night sky.

✨ **Special session:** Hear firsthand from **Faria Mahmud**, Silver Medalist at the 2024 IOAA Junior, as she shares her inspiring journey competing on the international stage!

| Time | Session |
|---|---|
| 10:00 – 10:10 AM | Introduction to BDOAA & instructor |
| 10:10 – 10:20 AM | Syllabus & topic overview |
| 10:20 – 11:10 AM | Hands-on skymap session |
| 11:10 – 11:30 AM | Break |
| 11:30 – 12:00 PM | Previous international participant experience (with Faria Mahmud) |
| 12:00 – 1:00 PM | Celestial mechanics (building physical intuition) |
| 1:00 – 2:30 PM | Lunch break |
| 2:30 – 4:00 PM | Observational astronomy & introduction to cosmology (with Labib Bin Azad) |

### Day 2, Saturday, 11 July

Led by **Mohtasiba Hossain & Fairooz Shaira**. Day two goes deeper into the physics and mathematics of the cosmos. Participants will master how astronomers map out the sky, measure stellar brightness, and analyze cosmic radiation.

| Time | Session |
|---|---|
| 10:00 – 11:30 AM | Celestial coordinate systems (learning to navigate the sky) |
| 11:30 – 12:30 PM | Magnitude (the language of stellar brightness) |
| 12:30 – 1:30 PM | Lunch break |
| 1:30 – 2:30 PM | Radiation mechanics |
| 2:30 – 4:00 PM | Photometry (connecting theory to the measurement of starlight) |
