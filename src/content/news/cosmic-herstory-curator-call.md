Durbin invites applications from creative and experienced individuals to curate **Cosmic HerStory**, an exhibition celebrating the contributions, journeys and achievements of women in astronomy, at Independent University, Bangladesh from **26 November to 5 December 2026**. The exhibition is part of Durbin's [Cosmic HerStory](/news/british-council-bd) project.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and the call closes once a curator is selected, so please apply early. The honorarium is BDT 20,000–30,000, negotiable.

**[Read the full call and apply on cassa.bd →](https://cassa.bd/opportunities/2026-11-01-a)**

For queries, write to [durbin.cassa@iub.edu.bd](mailto:durbin.cassa@iub.edu.bd).
